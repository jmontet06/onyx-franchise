03 · The founders

We built this because
the room was wrong.

Reformers make you turn away from your coach. Pilates is precision work. Turning away costs you form, safety, and results. So Jonathan built a reformer you face forward on. Then he met Liz and Christian. Liz danced her whole life and found Pilates at 14. Christian played in the NFL and used Pilates to stay in it. Together we opened ONYX in a former heated yoga studio. One day, Christian got curious and turned up the heat in the room. Heated reformer started there, by accident, from a design decision we made for a different reason. We are Black and gay founders. We built a room where people who never felt welcome in fitness walk in and stay. That is not marketing. It is the reason the business works.

09 · The questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost to open?

$285,691 to $624,043. The range depends on your space and your build. Item 7 of the FDD has the line items.

What do I need financially?

$150,000 liquid. $500,000 net worth.

Do you offer financing?

No. We can point you to lenders who know franchise deals.

How much can I make?

Item 19 of the FDD covers financial performance. Request it and read it with your accountant.

Can I own more than one?

Open one. Run it well. Then we talk. It keeps our system strong.

Can I hire a manager and stay out of it?

ONYX is not an absentee franchise model. Your first year, you run the show. A manager comes later, with our approval. Community does not scale from an office.

Do I need Pilates experience?

No. You need to have a passion for building community and be willing to be in the room.

How long until I open?

Roughly 9 months from signing. Real estate and permits set the pace.

Where are you awarding?

Dense, walkable urban markets. Few studios per city. Ask about yours.

Do I get an exclusive territory?

You get a defined territory. Not an exclusive one. We place few studios per city because density protects your business, not because a contract says so. Item 12 has the specifics.

What makes you different from Club Pilates or JETSET?

They sell you a system. We partner on a studio. They award hundreds a year. We will award a handful. Heat and reformer in one room is ours.

Why should I bet on a brand with 2 studios?

Because you get founders on the phone instead of a call center, and a market nobody has crowded yet. If you want 1,400 locations of proof, buy one of those.

What if my market is not on the list?

Tell us anyway. We are choosing markets now.
